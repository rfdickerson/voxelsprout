# The Imperial City (Oblivion) -- an orbit of the White-Gold Tower.
#
# Engine space: x east, y height, z south. This tour flies the ICImperialPalace
# WORLDSPACE, not Tamriel, and that is the whole trick to finding this place:
#
#   THE IMPERIAL CITY IS NOT IN TAMRIEL. Its nine districts are nine separate
#   worldspaces -- ICImperialPalace, ICMarketDistrict, ICTempleDistrict,
#   ICTalosPlazaDistrict, ICArboretumDistrict, ICElvenGardensDistrict,
#   ICArenaDistrict, ICImperialPrisonDistrict, ICTheArcaneUniversity -- and
#   Tamriel's own cell (0,0) is a landless dumping ground with 1977 references
#   in it, which is exactly convincing enough to waste an afternoon on.
#
# Nor do the districts sit near their own origin: ICImperialPalace occupies grid
# x [-1,8] z [-1,16] and its content is densest at (6,15). Probing (0,0) finds
# nothing and looks like an empty worldspace. `--cellindex <plugin> <worldspace>`
# now prints occupied bounds and the densest cells for exactly this reason.
#
# Grid (6,15) is engine x 24576..28672, z -65536..-61440, terrain a flat plateau
# at 4016..4128. The ring wall is centred near (26624, -63488) and the tour
# orbits that point from the south-west round to the east.
#
# THE RADIUS IS CONSTANT AT 17000, and that was a correction. The first cut
# closed from 17000 to 11000 over the orbit, which reads well as a plan and
# frames badly as a picture: by the end the tower subtends more than the frame
# and the shot crops BOTH the spire and the dome, which is the one framing that
# shows neither the whole silhouette nor a detail worth being close to. This
# subject is a single object 15000 units tall, so distance IS the framing, and
# the motion is carried by the azimuth sweep and the 2400-unit climb instead.
#
# THE AIM POINT IS 5000 UNITS UP THE TOWER, not on the ground. The tower is tall
# enough to leave frame entirely; aiming at its base points the camera at the
# plateau and crops the spire, and aiming at the spire tips the dome and the ring
# wall out of shot. (26624, ~9000, -63488) holds the whole silhouette -- spire,
# shaft, dome, wall -- from every waypoint here.
#
# Late afternoon. Sun elevation is cos(hour/24*2pi)*75 with negative meaning
# ABOVE the horizon, so ODAI_FNV_HOUR=16 puts it ~37 degrees up.
#
#   odai_game_newvegas --stream "<Oblivion/Data>" --plugin Oblivion.esm \
#     --worldspace ICImperialPalace --tour-file assets/tours/imperial_city.txt \
#     --flythrough 34 --capture-video imperial_city.mp4 30 20
#
# The flythrough deliberately OUTLASTS the capture -- 34 seconds of path for 20
# recorded. The flythrough clock runs during capture warm-up, and warm-up waits
# on streaming, so a path timed to the capture parks before the last frame. See
# assets/tours/bravil.txt, where that was shot wrong first.
#
# A district worldspace is a small island: past its occupied cells the terrain
# ends in pale flat sheets over nothing. Keeping the camera inside radius ~17000
# and above the plateau keeps them out of frame.
