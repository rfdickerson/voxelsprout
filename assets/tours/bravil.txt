# Bravil (Oblivion) -- in across the Niben, then up and along the curtain wall.
#
# Engine space: x east, y height, z south. Bravil sits on the water in southern
# Cyrodiil, and finding it was not a matter of reading its worldspace record:
# BravilWorld's NAM0/NAM9 corners span 52 cells of Niben Bay rather than a city
# footprint, unlike Skingrad's, which framed its city exactly. What located it
# was reference density -- the content clusters at Tamriel cells x 17..23,
# y -44..-42 -- and then terrain height, which said most of those cells are
# BELOW SEA LEVEL. Cell (21,-43) runs -4064..-2112 and is open bay; only
# (19,-42) rises above 0. Bravil fronts that shore.
#
# So the approach is over water on purpose. It is the one thing this city has
# that Skingrad and Seyda Neen do not, and the wall reads as a silhouette across
# it from a long way out.
#
# The aim points sit around (89900, 700, 155700), which is roughly the Chapel of
# Mara's spire -- the tallest thing in the city and the only feature that stays
# legible from the far side of the bay. Far-ahead aim points for the reason the
# Seyda Neen tour records: a close target is a steep pitch, and a steep pitch
# over a walled city is a picture of parapet tops.
#
# Late afternoon. Sun elevation is cos(hour/24*2pi)*75 with negative meaning
# ABOVE the horizon, so ODAI_FNV_HOUR=16 puts it ~37 degrees up.
#
#   odai_game_newvegas --stream "<Oblivion/Data>" --plugin Oblivion.esm \
#     --worldspace Tamriel --tour-file assets/tours/bravil.txt \
#     --flythrough 22 --capture-video bravil.mp4 30 22
#
# Add ODAI_FNV_DEBUGVIEW=ao for the clay-render version. Note water and sky are
# NOT covered by the debug views -- only imported_static.frag.slang is -- so the
# bay comes through flat grey there rather than occluded.
#
