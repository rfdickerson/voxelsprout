# Skingrad (Oblivion) -- the approach from the east, along the ridge above the
# Gold Road, ending three-quarter across the rooftops.
#
# Engine space: x east, y height, z south. Skingrad sits inside the bounds its
# own worldspace record declares (SkingradWorld NAM0/NAM9, parent Tamriel):
# beth X -81920..-53248, beth Y -12288..20480, i.e. engine x -81920..-53248 and
# engine z -20480..12288. The city centre is around engine (-64000, -4200) with
# terrain at 5800-7400, so every height here is 700-1500 units above ground --
# roughly 10-20 m at Oblivion's ~70 units per metre.
#
# Beats, and each one was framed with a pinned --screenshot before it was
# written down rather than guessed:
#   1-3  low over the ridge, rocks and grass passing close for parallax
#   4-5  the reveal: the Chapel of Julianos tower clears the ridge line
#   6-8  drift north, off the gate axis, and rise onto the rooftops
#
# Shoot it in late afternoon. The sun's elevation is cos(hour/24*2pi)*75 with
# negative being ABOVE the horizon, so ODAI_FNV_HOUR=16 puts it ~37 degrees up
# and warm, which is what makes the tower shadows and the stone read.
