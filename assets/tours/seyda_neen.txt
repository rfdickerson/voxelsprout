# Seyda Neen (Morrowind) -- in off the marsh, along the village, up and back.
#
# Engine space: x east, y height, z south. Seyda Neen is Vvardenfell cell
# (-2,-9), which is beth X -16384..-8192 and beth Y -73728..-65536, i.e. engine
# x -16384..-8192 and engine z 65536..73728. MORROWIND CELLS ARE 8192 UNITS, not
# the 4096 every later game uses, so a coordinate that looks like two cells over
# is one. Terrain in the cell runs -872..1480 and the village sits near 0..500,
# which is why these heights look small beside the Oblivion tour's.
#
# Water is at Z=0 (the cell's own XCLW), so the marsh under the opening beats is
# a real water surface rather than terrain.
#
# THE AIM POINTS ARE DELIBERATELY FAR AHEAD -- 2500 to 5800 units. The first cut
# of this path aimed 1100 units out, and the camera spent the middle of the shot
# staring almost straight down into roofs and tree canopies from ten metres up.
# A close aim point is a steep pitch, and a steep pitch over a village is a
# picture of rooftops. Pushing the target out flattens the pitch to 18-25
# degrees without moving the camera at all.
#
# The aim rotates NW -> W -> SW across the eight waypoints so the turn is
# carried by the lookAt curve rather than by a heading change at one waypoint,
# which is what whips.
#
#   odai_game_newvegas --stream "<Morrowind/Data Files>" --plugin Morrowind.esm \
#     --worldspace Vvardenfell --tour-file assets/tours/seyda_neen.txt \
#     --flythrough 20 --capture-video seyda_neen.mp4 30 20
#
